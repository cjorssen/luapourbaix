luapourbaix is a generic PGFPlots library for constructing limiting
potential-pH (Pourbaix) diagrams of aqueous chemical systems with LuaTeX.
It works with plain LuaTeX, LuaLaTeX and ConTeXt MkIV.

Declare the species, temperature, total element concentration and boundary
convention. The library checks and completes thermodynamic data from a sourced
offline catalogue, calculates boundaries and reconstructs the retained domains,
including disproportionation. PGF keys expose constants and boundary equations.

Features include water stability overlays, configurable colors and TikZ patterns,
monochrome diagrams, classification pictures and automatic reports. The manual
contains fully executable tutorials on iron, iodine and uranium. The model is
intended for teaching solution chemistry; it is not a full speciation solver.
